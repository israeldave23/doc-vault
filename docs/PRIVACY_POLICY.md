# Privacy Policy — Document Vault

**Last updated:** October 2026 · DGA Studios · Contact: (add your support email)

Document Vault ("the app") is an offline document-expiry reminder for IDs,
licenses and permits. This policy explains what the app does with your data.

## What the app does not do

- It has **no accounts and no server**. There is no sign-up, no login and no
  backend that stores your information.
- It **never uploads your documents, photos, ID numbers or notes**. Document
  images are encrypted with AES-256 on your device and stay on your device.
- It makes **no analytics or tracking network calls of its own**.

## Data stored on your device

Everything the app holds lives in encrypted local storage on your phone:
document records (title, category, expiry date, optional ID number and
notes), people/profiles, settings, and the encrypted photo files. Removing the
app removes that data.

## Backups

The only file that leaves the phone is a backup **you** choose to export. It
is encrypted with a passphrase you set (PBKDF2 + AES-256); without the
passphrase it cannot be opened. The app never transmits it anywhere.

## Advertising (free tier only)

The free tier shows one banner ad on the dashboard, delivered by **Google
AdMob**. AdMob collects the advertising identifier and pseudonymous data to
serve and measure ads, per [Google's policies](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)
and the [Google Privacy & Terms](https://policies.google.com/privacy) page.
Pro removes ads entirely, which also removes this processing.

AdMob is the only component in the app that uses the network.

## Permissions

- **Camera / photos** — to capture document images.
- **Notifications** — to remind you before a document expires.
- **Biometrics** — to unlock the app; fingerprints/faces never leave the
  device and are read through the system prompt only.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.
